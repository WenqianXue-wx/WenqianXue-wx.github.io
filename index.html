University of Florida

My research focuses on safe, reliable, and interpretable learning and control for autonomous aerial, robotic, and multi-agent systems under uncertainty, including UAVs and robotic manipulator systems. My work pursues two complementary directions: AI for Control, which develops learning-based methods for adaptive and safe control and objective and intent inference; and Control for AI, which develops control-theoretic foundations for reliable generative and sequential learning, including safe trajectory and distribution generation. Specifically, my research lies at the intersection of automatic control, (inverse) reinforcement learning, deep learning, game theory, and generative modeling.

Currently, I am a Postdoctoral Associate at the Nonlinear Control and Robotics Laboratory (NCR), Department of Mechanical and Aerospace Engineering, University of Florida. I am also an Adjunct Professor in the Department of Electrical and Computer Engineering and I teach Linear Control Systems. Before joining the NCR Laboratory in 2024, I was a Postdoctoral Associate at the Computational NeuroEngineering Laboratory, Department of Electrical and Computer Engineering, University of Florida. I received my Ph.D. in Electrical Engineering in 2022 and my M.S.E. in Control Engineering in 2018 from Northeastern University, China.

Research Directions

  • Adaptive and Safe learning-Based Control: Data-driven reinforcement learning and deep learning methods for adaptive, safe, robust, interpretable control to enable autonomous systems to act reliably under uncertain dynamics and imperfect information, including measurement errors and limited observations.
  • Objective and Intent Inference: Data-driven, model-free inverse reinforcement learning method to infer underlying objectives from demonstrations with certain system dynamics and imperfect observations to enable autonomous systems to understand observed behavior and perform trajectory imitation and guidance.
  • Representation and Generative Learning: Control-theoretic representation learning and generative modeling methods with guaranteed convergence to enable autonomous systems to extract, recover, and safely generate informative representations from data, including distribution and trajectory generation.
  • Implementations: My research has been applied to autonomous aerial and robotic systems, including UAVs/drones platforms, robotic manipulator systems, and multi-agent systems.
