My research interests focus on advancing intelligent, efficient, safe, and explainable learning and control methodologies for autonomous, robotic, and multi-agent systems, including learning-based control methods through policy seeking and objective reconstruction (AI for control) and control theory-inspired generative modeling methods (Control for AI) for distribution charaterization.

01 — About

Control theory, shaped by data.

My research lies at the intersection of control theory, reinforcement learning, game theory, and deep learning. I am especially interested in inverse reinforcement learning and adaptive dynamic programming for systems that must learn reliably from limited observations.

I received my Ph.D. in Control Theory and Control Engineering in 2022 and my M.S.E. in Control Engineering in 2018 from Northeastern University, China. Before joining the NCR Laboratory in 2024, I was a Postdoctoral Associate at the University of Florida’s Computational NeuroEngineering Laboratory.

02 — Research

Research directions

01

Inverse Reinforcement Learning

Recovering objectives and control policies from demonstrated behavior, including hidden-state, robust, and networked settings.

02

Learning-Based Control

Data-driven and model-free algorithms with stability, robustness, and convergence guarantees.

03

Multi-Agent Systems

Graphical games, distributed control, clustering, consensus, and strategic interaction across networked agents.

04

Adaptive Intelligence

Deep learning and adaptive dynamic programming for nonlinear systems, robotics, and safety-critical autonomy.
